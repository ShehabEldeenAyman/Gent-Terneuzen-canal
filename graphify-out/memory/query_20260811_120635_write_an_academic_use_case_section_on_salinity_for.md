# Q: Write an academic use-case section on salinity forecasting in the Gent-Terneuzen Canal, including sensors, locations, time lags, spatial gradients, alerts, long-term change, and cross-border reporting

## Answer

The use case comprises conductivity monitoring at Zevenaarshaven (IOW51, Terneuzen), Autrichehaven (IOW50, Westdorpe), Rodenhuizedok (IOW49, Ghent), and Grootdok (IOW48, Ghent), together with the Water-Link Induss II industrial target. Their north-to-south placement reveals inland salt propagation. Earlier observations at northern stations form time-lagged predictors for later target salinity, while simultaneous differences between locations form spatial gradients that describe the salt front. Forecasts can provide operational warning, support industrial water management, track long-term change, and improve Belgian-Dutch reporting.
